NarraFork remote-peer: independent distribution third-party materials

Scope: Go 1.26.1 standard library (including its bundled vendor packages) and all 23 modules required by remote-peer/go.mod, as locked by go.sum.
These components are compiled into the separately downloaded remote-peer, not embedded in the NarraFork main executable.
Test-only modules appearing only in go.sum (testify and yaml) are not linked or distributed.
CGO is disabled. BoringSSL, Go build tools, Pion test-wasm/examples/testdata are not part of these six peer binaries.

All license and notice bodies below are copied verbatim from the installed Go 1.26.1 source or locked module source cache. Exact byte-identical materials are explicitly mapped to their first full-text section, without merging different authors or years. SHA-256 identifies each original body or indicated original source-line excerpt.
The additional Go-authored Pion and anet notices accompany the complete Go BSD license; the fiat-crypto notices accompany the standard-library notice.

Review note: Pion interceptor/pkg/twcc/arrival_time_map.go declares MIT but describes an adaptation of Chrome/WebRTC. The module supplies no separate WebRTC notice; this upstream attribution question remains subject to review, not a claim of independently verified legal compliance.
